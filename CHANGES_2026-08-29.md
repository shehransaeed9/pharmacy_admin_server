# What changed in this update

## Matches the customer-app changes
See `CHANGES_2026-08-27.md` in the customer app package for the paired
fix on that side (the Settings-save crash, subscription tab, etc). The
changes here are what make that possible on the admin side:

## 1. Multiple subscription plans
`/admin/settings` now has 3 plan blocks (label / days / amount each —
leave a label blank to offer only 1 or 2 plans). These are what
`/api/pricing` and `/api/checkin` send to every pharmacy's app, so
pharmacies pick a real plan instead of seeing one flat fee.

## 2. Add Pharmacy
New page (`+ Add Pharmacy` in the nav) to create a pharmacy account
directly and generate its license key — for phone/in-person sign-ups.
Give the pharmacy that key; they enter it on their app's Setup screen
under "Already have a license key?" instead of self-registering, and
they land on whatever plan/expiry you chose here instead of a fresh
free trial.

## 3. Unsubscribe
Each pharmacy row now has a clear "Unsubscribe" button (replacing the
generic Disable toggle) — immediately expires their subscription AND
locks their app on its next check-in, with a "Resubscribe" to restore
access. The Extend control also now offers your configured plans as a
quick-pick alongside the exact-days field.

## 4. Professional front end
Both the admin panel and the customer app were re-themed: a consistent
color system, Inter typeface, proper page headers and stat cards,
polished buttons/tables/badges, and cleaner copy throughout (e.g.
"Unsubscribed" instead of "Disabled", "Sign In" instead of "Log In").
Nothing structural changed — same routes, same data — so this is a
safe drop-in over your existing `instance/license_server.db`.
